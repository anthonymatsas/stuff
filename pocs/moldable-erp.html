Keep this workspace

Exploring is loose. A kept workspace runs on a schedule, so anything relative has to resolve to something that still means the same thing next month.

What's real here, and what isn't

Real

  • The workspace spec is a real data structure. Every turn is a list of typed operations applied to it, and the canvas is a pure render of the result.
  • Selective revert is real. Turning a turn off replays the remaining ops from the seed — it isn't an undo stack, so you can disable turn 2 and keep turn 3.
  • The numbers are computed, not fixtures. A synthetic catalogue of 18 SKUs across 6 DCs is projected forward daily and re-aggregated whenever scope, window or grain changes.
  • Block-level targeting is real. Scoping a selected block writes an override on that block only, which is why the chart and table can disagree.

Faked

  • The language understanding. This is a pattern matcher over a fixed vocabulary, not a model. It handles a few hundred plausible phrasings and says so when it misses. A real system would use an LLM here, and that mapping is the actual technical risk this prototype cannot test.
  • Nothing persists — reload and you're back to the seed.

What to watch in a session

  • Does the second turn land? Refining beats re-describing, or the premise is dead.
  • Does anyone read the interpretation strip before believing the chart?
  • Does anyone select a block on purpose, or do they only ever talk to the whole canvas?
  • At Keep, does anyone notice the relative window has to be resolved?
